About

About me

I study how AI generates, understands, and acts—and when we can trust it to do so.

I am an AI Engineer at AI-MAGE, working on agents and AGI. I received my PhD in Systems and Control Engineering from Institute of Science Tokyo, with research spanning generative AI security, trustworthy machine learning, and interpretability.
