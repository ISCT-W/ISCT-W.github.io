01 / ABOUT

About me

I study how AI generates, understands, and acts—and when we can trust it to do so.

I hold a PhD in Systems and Control Engineering from Institute of Science Tokyo. My research focuses on generative AI security, trustworthy machine learning, and interpretability. I also work on agent and multimodal application systems.
